About BrahmnMitra

One partner for flights, tours and MICE

BrahmnMitra is a New Delhi travel and tourism solutions provider working across both directions of the map — booking domestic and international flights, crafting curated journeys through India, and running corporate MICE programmes end to end.

For travellers coming to India we build the itinerary: royal palaces to spiritual retreats, vibrant cities to untouched landscapes. For companies and families going out of India we handle the fares, the routing and the logistics — and we pick up the phone when something changes at 2am.

Our team combines cultural insight, logistical precision, and personalized service to deliver exceptional travel solutions for groups, families, corporates, and special interest travelers.
