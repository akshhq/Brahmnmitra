✦ End-to-End Concierge Workflow

How we craft your journey.

Great journeys do not happen by chance — they are engineered through thoughtful listening, meticulous route design, and 24/7 operational vigilance.

01
Step 1 · Initial Discovery

Consultation & Travel Blueprint

We begin by understanding who is traveling, your preferred pace, dietary preferences, mobility requirements, and past travel highlights. Whether you are traveling as a couple, multi-generational family, or a 50-person corporate leadership team, we establish a crystal-clear travel brief.

• Travel Pace Assessment • Special Interest Alignments • Budget Optimization
02
Step 2 · Bespoke Curation

Route Design & Accommodation Pairing

Our travel designers map out realistic transit times, handpick boutique or 5-star properties, and secure optimal flight connections. We eliminate rushed itineraries, ensuring adequate leisure time for genuine cultural absorption.

• Handpicked Heritage & Boutique Stays • Realistic Transit Pacing • Local Guide & Activity Curation
03
Step 3 · Full Transparency

Itemized Quotation & Reservation Lock

You receive a comprehensive, itemized quotation with clear inclusions, hotel room categories, vehicle types, and full GST SAC 9985 tax breakdown. No hidden surcharges, no surprise fees. Upon approval, seats and rooms are locked immediately.

• SAC 9985 GST Tax Invoices • Clear Advance / Balance Split • Transparent Cancellation Policies
04
Step 4 · Pre-Departure Care

Travel Dossier & Web Check-In

72 hours prior to departure, you receive your complete digital travel kit: confirmed e-tickets, hotel booking vouchers, chauffeur contact numbers, regional weather advisories, and emergency escalation lines. We handle web check-ins and boarding passes on your behalf.

• Digital Itinerary & Vouchers • Automated Web Check-In • Driver & Guide Details in Advance
05
Step 5 · On-Ground Vigilance

24/7 Dedicated Concierge Sentinel

Throughout your trip, our Delhi operations team is actively monitoring flight movements, hotel check-ins, and road conditions. If a flight is delayed or you wish to adjust a day's schedule on the fly, one WhatsApp message or phone call handles it immediately.

• Real-Time Flight Sentinel • Instant WhatsApp / Call Support • On-the-Fly Itinerary Tweaks
06
Step 6 · Post-Trip Relationship

Debrief, Feedback & Loyalty Care

We connect post-journey to gather your feedback, record personal preferences for future journeys, and ensure complete billing and invoice reconciliations for corporate accounts.

• Customer Experience Debrief • Traveler Preference Profiling • Loyalty Privileges on Next Trip
