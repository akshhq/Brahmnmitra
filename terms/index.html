1. Introduction & Nature of Services

BrahmnMitra operates as a dedicated travel agency and tour operator based in New Delhi, India. We act as an intermediary coordinating with airlines, certified destination management companies (DMCs), hotels, and transport providers to curate, book, and deliver tailor-made domestic and international journeys.

2. Quotations, Pricing & Bookings

  • Guidance vs. Confirmed Pricing: Starting prices shown on our website or marketing channels are indicative estimates. Final prices are established only upon written quotation detailing exact dates, room categories, and airline fare classes.
  • Airfare Volatility: Flight fares, seat availability, and airport taxes fluctuate continuously until tickets are formally issued. Quotations remain subject to airline fare rule revisions prior to ticketing payment receipt.
  • Deposit & Confirmation: A booking is formally confirmed once the required deposit (or full payment, depending on the service timeline) is credited and acknowledged via written booking voucher.

3. Traveler Responsibilities: Passports, Visas & Health

  • Passport Validity: Travelers on international sectors must possess a valid passport with at least six (6) months validity remaining from their scheduled return date.
  • Visa Compliance: While BrahmnMitra provides guidance and document preparation assistance, visa grant decisions are at the sole discretion of the respective embassy or consulate. Travelers are responsible for adhering to destination entry criteria.
  • Health & Insurance: We strongly advise all clients to procure comprehensive international travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip interruptions, and baggage loss.
