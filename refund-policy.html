Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026. We believe in total clarity regarding cancellations, date modifications, airline penalties, and refund turnaround times.

1. Airline Ticket Cancellations & Modifications

Flight bookings are governed directly by individual airline tariff rules and DGCA / international aviation carriage conventions:

  • Refundable vs. Non-Refundable Fares: Basic promotional fares are often non-refundable or incur high airline penalties. Flexible fare classes permit changes subject to fare difference and airline modification charges.
  • Airline Schedule Changes / Cancellations: If an airline cancels a flight or alters timings by more than 3 hours, you are entitled to full airline fare refunds or alternate sector routing according to carrier rules.
  • Agency Processing: BrahmnMitra charges a standard administrative fee of ₹500 per passenger per sector for processing voluntary airline cancellation or rescheduling requests.

2. Package & Tailor-Made Tour Cancellations

Because custom holiday packages involve pre-booked hotels, private drivers, and reserved sightseeing permits, the following cancellation fee schedule applies to land package components (excluding non-refundable airline tickets):

Cancellation Notice Received Applicable Retention / Fee Refund Eligible
30 or more days before departure 15% of total package cost (or non-refundable deposit) 85% of land package
15 to 29 days before departure 35% of total package cost 65% of land package
7 to 14 days before departure 60% of total package cost 40% of land package
Less than 7 days / No-Show 100% of package cost 0% (Non-refundable)

*Special peak-season bookings (e.g., Diwali, Christmas/New Year, regional festivals) and safari/luxury train permits may carry 100% non-refundable supplier terms as specified in your quotation voucher.

3. Non-Refundable Services

  • Visa application and embassy biometric processing fees once submitted.
  • Travel insurance policy premiums once the certificate is generated.
  • Specific wildlife safari permits, luxury rail permits, and cruise deposits.

4. Refund Turnaround Timeline

Once an eligible cancellation is verified and approved, refunds are credited back to the original source bank account or payment method within 7 to 14 banking days. A confirmation receipt and credit voucher will be emailed upon remittance.
