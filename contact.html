Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about booking, turnaround times, and how our travel specialists work with you.

How quickly will a travel specialist respond to my inquiry?

For messages sent via WhatsApp or telephone during business hours (Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST), we typically respond within 10–15 minutes. For online form submissions and email inquiries, you will receive an initial consultation response within 2–4 business hours.

Can I customize an existing package or build a completely bespoke itinerary?

Yes, absolutely. All packages displayed on BrahmnMitra are starting outlines. Our travel experts tailor every day-by-day itinerary to your preferred pace, hotel categories, flight schedules, dietary needs, and private sightseeing choices.

Do you arrange corporate offsites, conferences (MICE), and large group bookings?

Yes. We have dedicated corporate desk specialists who handle group airline ticketing, block hotel bookings with conference facilities, private airport transfers, event logistics, and GST-compliant invoicing (SAC 9985).

What payment methods do you accept and how does booking confirmation work?

We accept bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), UPI/QR, and major credit/debit cards. Typically, bookings are secured with an initial milestone deposit (e.g. 25–50%), with the remaining balance settled according to airline and hotel supplier deadlines. All receipts and vouchers are delivered digitally.

Do you assist with visas, travel insurance, and flight seat selection?

Yes. Our flight and concierge team assists with visa documentation guidance, comprehensive international travel insurance, preferred seat selection, extra baggage allowances, and special meal requests.
